\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Rotation in Z-\/axis (right-\/hand rule\+: thumb along +Z) 

Concrete implementation of \doxylink{classRotationMatrix}{Rotation\+Matrix} for rotations in the Z-\/axis. Common for waist/azimuth joints and wrist yaw in robotic manipulators.
