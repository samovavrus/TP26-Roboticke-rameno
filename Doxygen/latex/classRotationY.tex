Rotation in Y-\/axis (right-\/hand rule\+: thumb along +Y) 

Concrete implementation of \doxylink{classRotationMatrix}{Rotation\+Matrix} for rotations in the Y-\/axis. Common for elbow and wrist pitch joints in robotic manipulators.
